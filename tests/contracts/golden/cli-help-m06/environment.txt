Remote runner environments: add, invite, join, revoke-client, remove, update, status (M06; ADR-0012 §3, §7)

Usage: heimdall environment [OPTIONS] <COMMAND>

Commands:
  add            Bootstrap a runner over your existing, host-verified access: one interactive ssh session in which the runner's `heimdall remote init` enrolls this device's management key as owner, confirmed at the runner. Also repairs a lost pin or key
  invite         Invite another device's management key to a runner. You confirm its fingerprint here; the runner holds the invitation until it is confirmed there
  join           On the invited device: join from the owner's invitation bundle after confirming the runner's host key and environment ID. `--show-key` prints this device's management key for the owner first
  revoke-client  Revoke one client on a runner (narrowing; no decision)
  remove         Revoke this device on a runner, then forget the runner. The management key is kept
  update         Ask a runner for its explicit, checksum-verified binary update
  recheck        Recheck a runner that went owner-only: probe a real management session to it and send the runner what the probes observed. Owner only. Nothing is lifted here: the runner's owner lifts the downgrade at the runner, within 15 minutes
  status         This controller's view of its runners. Opens no connection
  help           Print this message or the help of the given subcommand(s)

Options:
      --json             Machine output: one JSON envelope on stdout (NDJSON for event streams)
      --non-interactive  Never open a browser, dialog, or terminal prompt (spec §11.3). Owner decisions then exit 3 with `next.decision_ref` and nothing is sent
      --home <DIR>       Override the Heimdall home directory (fixtures and tests) [env: HEIMDALL_HOME=]
  -h, --help             Print help
