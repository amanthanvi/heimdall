Choose what happens to owned connections at the deadline, and whether Heimdall owns a multiplex master (M03)

Usage: heimdall access connection-policy [OPTIONS] --policy <POLICY>

Options:
      --json
          Machine output: one JSON envelope on stdout (NDJSON for event streams)

      --policy <POLICY>
          Policy ID

      --existing <EXISTING>
          What happens to existing owned connections at the deadline

          Possible values:
          - graceful: Existing dedicated sessions may stay connected
          - strict:   Owned connections are closed at the deadline

      --non-interactive
          Never open a browser, dialog, or terminal prompt (spec §11.3). Owner decisions then exit 3 with `next.decision_ref` and nothing is sent

      --home <DIR>
          Override the Heimdall home directory (fixtures and tests)
          
          [env: HEIMDALL_HOME=]

      --multiplexing <MULTIPLEXING>
          Whether Heimdall owns a multiplex master per pair

          Possible values:
          - disabled: No master
          - owned:    One owned master per pair

  -h, --help
          Print help (see a summary with '-h')
