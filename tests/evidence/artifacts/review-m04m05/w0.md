## Findings

### W0-R01 — Blocking (ADR-0009 §2.1): Workspace `lock_behavior` leaks onto personal and cross-workspace grants' durable `GrantOrigin` records

- **Severity:** **Blocking** (ADR-0009 §2.1: lasting effect on durable recovery/authority records (`grants.origin_json`) and irreversible revocation of active personal grants on lock, triggered by the user's own ordinary action; violates ADR-0011 §10 item 1 and ADR-0006 §17.6 R5-Q9).
- **Location:** [`crates/app/src/operations/lock_connection_m03.rs:125-144`](crates/app/src/operations/lock_connection_m03.rs#L125-L144) (specifically line 143); callers in [`crates/app/src/operations.rs:6420`](crates/app/src/operations.rs#L6420) and [`crates/app/src/operations.rs:7079`](crates/app/src/operations.rs#L7079).
- **Mechanism:** `with_lock_and_connection` loads **all** standing policies for `(origin.profile, environment)` via `tx.policies_for(&origin.profile, environment)?` (line 125). It filters those to the grant's context when computing `connection` (`merged_policy(&policies, &crate::policy_context::grant_context(Some(&origin)))`, lines 126–129), but line 143 passes the **unfiltered** `&policies` slice to `crate::lock::origin_behavior(&policies, floor)`.
- **Concrete scenario:**
  1. A user has a personal policy (`Continue`) and a workspace `ws1` policy (`RevokeOnLock`) for profile `dev`.
  2. The user performs an ordinary personal `ensure` (or `decide` approval, or work in workspace `ws2`).
  3. `with_lock_and_connection` writes `origin.lock_behavior = Some(LockBehavior::RevokeOnLock)` into SQLite `grants.origin_json` via write-once `set_grant_origin`. Even if `ws1`'s policy is later modified or deleted, that `Some(RevokeOnLock)` remains permanently pinned in the personal grant's `GrantOrigin`.
  4. When the screen locks (`on_session_lock` in [`crates/app/src/operations/lock.rs:44-60`](crates/app/src/operations/lock.rs#L44-L60)), `effective_behavior_with_floor` ([`crates/app/src/lock.rs:125`](crates/app/src/lock.rs#L125)) reads `origin.lock_behavior` and permanently revokes the personal grant (`Revoked` + tombstone), and `reissue` refuses re-issuance after restart.
- **Probe & exit code:** Temporary probe `w0_r01_probe_workspace_lock_behavior_leaks_into_personal_grant_origin` in `tests/model/operations.rs` (command: `cargo test --locked -p heimdall-runtime --test model_operations w0_r01_probe`, subsequently reverted):
  - Result: **exit 101** (`FAILED`)
  - Output: `assertion left == right failed: personal grant origin polluted by workspace policy: origin.lock_behavior=Some(RevokeOnLock), revoked_on_lock=[GrantId("grant_6bf089cdb9ac4153ea8c790d06adb3ce")], state_after_lock=Revoked`.

### W0-R02 — Non-blocking: `Operations::applicable_policies` leaks workspace policies into `lock_disclosure` and `inspect capabilities`

- **Severity:** **Non-blocking** (display/disclosure only; no lasting effect on remote trust, recovery records, or owner data, and no consent bypass).
- **Location:** [`crates/app/src/lock.rs:408-420`](crates/app/src/lock.rs#L408-L420) (`Operations::applicable_policies`), called by `lock_disclosure` ([`crates/app/src/lock.rs:437`](crates/app/src/lock.rs#L437)), `pending_lock_disclosure` ([`crates/app/src/lock.rs:456`](crates/app/src/lock.rs#L456)), and `add_capabilities_lock` ([`crates/app/src/operations/lock_connection_m03.rs:249`](crates/app/src/operations/lock_connection_m03.rs#L249)).
- **Mechanism:** `Operations::applicable_policies` collects `tx.policies_for(&profile.id, &env)?` without filtering to `PolicyContext::Personal` (only reads `self.environment()`, and includes all `workspace: Some(_)` policies).
- **Concrete scenario:** When a workspace policy has `RevokeOnLock` and the personal policy is `Continue`, `inspect capabilities` and `lock_disclosure` / `pending_lock_disclosure` report `behavior: "revoke_on_lock"` and Linux `signal_unverified` warnings as the runtime's general lock behaviour.
- **Probe & exit code:** Temporary probe `w0_r02_probe_workspace_lock_behavior_leaks_into_disclosure` in `tests/model/operations.rs` (`cargo test --locked -p heimdall-runtime --test model_operations w0_r02_probe`, reverted):
  - Result: **exit 101** (`FAILED`)
  - Output: `assertion left == right failed: personal lock_disclosure leaked workspace lock_behavior=RevokeOnLock, warnings=[Warning { code: "signal_unverified", ... }]`.

### W0-R03 — Non-blocking: `Operations::delegate` omits pre-admission check (writes rows on `Removed` environment) and breaks managed-environment coalescing

- **Severity:** **Non-blocking** (does not grant unauthorized authority, bypass consent, alter remote trust, or corrupt recovery records: `resolve_in` still admits/refuses inside `drive_work` and resolves in the parent's environment; leaves only a failed `operations`/`idempotency`/`events` row on a retired environment or an uncoalesced duplicate awaiting decision).
- **Locations:**

## Answers to the Review Questions

### 1. Authority, environments
- **Managed-set admission & cross-environment isolation:**
  - Pure admission [`admit_environment`](crates/domain/src/environment.rs#L155-L171) admits `target == own` or a `managed_environments` row whose `environment == target && controller == own && state.in_set()` (`Active | NotRunning`); `Removed | Gone` returns `EnvironmentAdmission::Removed`, and absent or foreign-controller rows return `EnvironmentAdmission::Unmanaged`.
  - Store wrapper [`crates/app/src/environments.rs:30-39`](crates/app/src/environments.rs#L30-L39) maps `Unmanaged` → `NOT_FOUND` (no reason) and `Removed` → `NOT_FOUND` (`environment_removed`).
  - Admission checks are present at every authority, work, activation, issuance, authorization, and event-snapshot path: `ensure` ([`crates/app/src/operations.rs:9395`](crates/app/src/operations.rs#L9395)), `bind` ([`operations.rs:9489`](crates/app/src/operations.rs#L9489)), `resolve_in` ([`operations.rs:4255,4293`](crates/app/src/operations.rs#L4255)), `drive_work` ([`operations.rs:6243`](crates/app/src/operations.rs#L6243)), `activation_refusal` ([`operations.rs:2177`](crates/app/src/operations.rs#L2177)), `revalidate_chain` ([`operations.rs:1558`](crates/app/src/operations.rs#L1558)), `issuance_check_read_only` ([`operations.rs:12500`](crates/app/src/operations.rs#L12500)), `Operations::admit` ([`operations.rs:8488,8543`](crates/app/src/operations.rs#L8488)), `authorize` ([`crates/app/src/operations/authorize.rs:219,235`](crates/app/src/operations/authorize.rs#L219-L235)), and `connections` ([`crates/app/src/operations/connections.rs:85`](crates/app/src/operations/connections.rs#L85)).
  - **Gap found (`W0-R03`):** `Operations::delegate` ([`operations.rs:9856-9896`](crates/app/src/operations.rs#L9856-L9896)) does not call `admit` before `run_work` (writing op/idempotency/event rows on a `Removed` environment before `resolve_in` refuses it), and `WorkSpec::from_request` hardcodes `own` so managed-environment `Delegate` decisions do not coalesce (`W0-R03`). Also note [`Operations::revoke`](crates/app/src/operations.rs#L10356) intentionally permits revoking existing grants even in a `Removed` environment (fail-closed).
- **Per-environment isolation (slots, grants, signers, recovery, cleanup):**
  - Grants & bindings store their target `environment`; `retire_managed_environment` transitions state and bumps authority generation so `revalidate_chain` / `tick` withdraws only that environment's grants and runs per-generation cleanup.
  - Signer endpoints and slot directories are separated per environment (`SignerEndpoint::Environment` vs `SignerEndpoint::ManagedEnvironment(env)`, `ManagedEffectsImpl::with_slot_root`, `SlotStore::binding_environment`). Note: `ManagedSlotTable::state` ([`crates/adapters/src/managed/effects.rs:1161`](crates/adapters/src/managed/effects.rs#L1161)) still reads `slot_root().environment` (a single-environment test/inspection helper; actual claims/clears use `binding_environment` and `slot.environment`).
- **Issuer validation and restart recovery per environment:**
  - Verified: [`issuance_check_read_only`](crates/app/src/operations.rs#L12500-L12506) (called by [`heimdall_app::issuance_ro::assess`](crates/app/src/issuance_ro.rs#L173-L209) and [`heimdall_issuer::check::verify_reservation`](crates/issuer/src/check.rs#L178-L214)) runs `environments::admit(tx, env, &grant.environment)` and reads policies for `(p, &grant.environment)` in `ctx.policy_context`, while [`key_id_within_ceiling`](crates/issuer/src/check.rs#L107-L119) validates `heimdall:<own>:<id>:<serial>` against `txn.self_environment()`. Restart recovery (`managed_reissue_pass` / `Operations::start`) iterates across the managed set and restores each admitted environment's grants/slots/endpoints independently (verified by `two_environments_ensure_issue_bind_sign_restart_unmanaged_refused_cleanup_isolated`).
- **Any old equality check removed without an equivalent admission check?** None found in authorization/issuance/runtime seams (aside from the pre-write `delegate` ordering defect `W0-R03a`).

### 2. Authority, policy resolution
- **Personal / workspace isolation (`scope`, `pause`, `cutoff`, `connection`, `lock_behavior`):**
  - Domain [`applicable_policies`](crates/domain/src/policy.rs#L213-L227) and [`resolve_policy`](crates/domain/src/policy.rs#L247-L270) take scope/window/revision from the target context's own policy (or personal if workspace has none) and merge pause/cutoff/lock/connection fail-closed over applicable policies only. Thus workspace scope/pause/cutoff/connection/lock_behavior cannot reach personal work or another workspace through `resolve_policy`, `resolve_continuing_policy`, `resolve_in`, `activation_refusal`, `authorize`, `revalidate_chain`, `issuance_check_read_only`, `pair_connection_policy`, or `narrowing::policy_reader` ([`crates/app/src/operations/narrowing.rs:1010-1035`](crates/app/src/operations/narrowing.rs#L1010-L1035)).
  - **HOWEVER (`W0-R01`, blocking):** [`with_lock_and_connection`](crates/app/src/operations/lock_connection_m03.rs#L143) passes unfiltered `&policies` to `crate::lock::origin_behavior(&policies, floor)`, leaking any workspace's `RevokeOnLock` onto personal and other-workspace grants' durable `GrantOrigin` records!
  - **AND (`W0-R02`, non-blocking):** [`Operations::applicable_policies`](crates/app/src/lock.rs#L408-L420) fails to filter to `PolicyContext::Personal`, leaking workspace `lock_behavior` into `lock_disclosure` and `inspect capabilities`.
- **Personal blocks on workspace work:** Personal pause, cutoff, stricter `lock_behavior`, and stricter `connection` policy apply to that profile's workspace work via `applicable_policies` extending with `p.workspace.is_none()` and fail-closed merging in `resolve_policy` / `effective_lock_behavior`.
- **`Ambiguous` / `Untrusted` matching:** [`PolicyContext::of_match`](crates/domain/src/policy.rs) (verified by `two_policy_trace_resolution_is_by_context_never_by_id`) maps only `Trusted { workspace }` and `Member { workspace }` to `PolicyContext::Workspace(workspace)`; `Ambiguous` and `Untrusted` map to `PolicyContext::Personal`, keeping workspace-only scope out of reach.
- **First-by-ID across contexts:** Only retained *within the same context* when multiple standing policies exist in one context (as permitted by ADR-0006 §17.6 R5-Q10). No cross-context first-by-ID path remains in `resolve_policy`, `Operations::resolve_in`, `revalidate_chain`, `issuance_check_read_only`, `activation_refusal`, `narrowing`, or `pair_connection_policy` (the remaining leakage in `W0-R01` / `W0-R02` is unfiltered slice folding, not first-by-ID).
