## 2. Wave 3 Findings

### `M06-W3-R01` — `transport disable` leaves client Iroh allowlist (`remote_client_endpoints`) intact
- **Severity:** **Blocking** (ADR-0009 §2.1: security/revocation contract broken on a live transport boundary; lasting remote-trust allowlist survives `transport disable`).
- **Location:** `crates/runtime/src/service.rs:959-965,1226-1231`; `crates/runtime/src/dispatch.rs:1862`; `crates/heimdall/src/m06_cmd.rs:1002`; `crates/app/src/transport.rs:647`.
- **Problem & Probe:** `TransportSupervisor` admits inbound remote peers via `StorePeerEndpoints` reading `tx.remote_client_endpoints()` (`crates/runtime/src/supervise_transport.rs:1308`, `crates/runtime/src/paired_endpoints.rs:24-33`), whereas both `RunnerTransport` (`service.rs:963`) and `RouteTransport::new` (`service.rs:1230`, `dispatch.rs:1862`, `m06_cmd.rs:1002`) pass `Arc::new(heimdall_app::transport::NoPeerEnrollments)`. Calling `TransportOps::disable` (`crates/app/src/transport.rs:647`) invokes `self.peers.clear()?`, returning `allowlist_entries_removed = 0` and leaving `remote_client_endpoints` populated. **Probe (`tests/integration/transport_supervise_m06.rs`, temporary `probe_service_route_transport_disable_leaks_allowlist`):** verified (`exit 0`) that `TransportRequest::Disable` left `1` endpoint in `read_authority` and re-enabling serve allowed the client to dial and connect over Iroh.
- **Smallest Fix:** Implement `heimdall_app::transport::PeerEnrollments` on `StorePeerEndpoints` (or a store adapter that clears `remote_client_endpoints` cleanly inside the narrowing/generation-bump write path) and wire it into `service.rs:963,1230`, `dispatch.rs:1862`, and `m06_cmd.rs:1002`.
- **Test Coverage Gap:** `integration_transport_supervise_m06.rs` tests `TransportSupervisor` admission in isolation without exercising `RouteTransport::disable` + `StorePeerEndpoints` allowlist clearing.

### `M06-W3-R02` — Paused/unknown-residual barrier bypassed if `PrepareRemoteGrant.policy` is omitted
- **Severity:** Nonblocking (W3/W4 boundary: `DeliveredGrant` in `crates/issuer/src/remote.rs:487-546` still enforces principals, extensions, `key_id`, `expires_at`, and barrier freshness (`outbox_pending`, `last_failure`, `last_report.at == binding.report_at`); `PreparedRemoteGrant.policy` is documented advisory until W4 controller-held policy record source (b) at `remote.rs:68-73,108-110`).
- **Location:** `crates/app/src/remote_status_driver.rs:616,627,692`; `crates/app/src/remote_status_barrier.rs:184-188,387-417`; `crates/issuer/src/check.rs:323`.
- **Problem & Probe:** `driver.rs` derives `policy` from `subject.policy` (carried in `PrepareRemoteGrant`) and passes `Option<&PolicyRef>` to `barrier.permit`. Inside `barrier.rs:387-417`, `report.policies` pause/cutoff and `policy:<id>` `policy_revisions` checks sit inside `if let Some(policy) = policy`. Additionally, `reconcile_in` (`barrier.rs:184-188`) skips `tx.insert_tombstone` for `CountedResidual::Unknown` even when `report.tombstones` carries a tombstone (which `issuer/src/check.rs:323` reads). **Probe (`tests/integration/controller_driver_m06.rs`, temporary `probe_compromised_runner_strips_policy_from_prepare_answer`):** verified (`exit 0`) that stripping `"policy"` bypassed a paused policy in `AuthorityReport.policies`, issued 2 `Published` certs (`binding.policy == None, cutoff == None`), and skipped `tx.insert_tombstone` for `Unknown`.
- **Smallest Fix:** Complete W4 record source (b) so policy/pause checks use controller-held policy records rather than runner-supplied `PrepareRemoteGrant.policy`, and record `report.tombstones` in `reconcile_in` regardless of `CountedResidual::Unknown`.
- **Test Coverage Gap:** No negative test covers stripped/mismatched `PrepareRemoteGrant.policy` against `AuthorityReport.policies` pauses or `Unknown` residual tombstones during `reconcile_in`.

### `M06-W3-R03` — `lift_downgrade` and `policy_recheck` ignore pending bridge downgrade markers
- **Severity:** Nonblocking (runtime still fails closed at `RunnerPolicy::current()` / `check_join` / `unverifiable()` at `crates/runtime/src/paired.rs:791-799`; causes inaccurate `lifted: true` reporting and immediate re-downgrade).
- **Location:** `crates/runtime/src/paired.rs:1148-1190,2335-2386` (vs `drain_pending` via `marker_dir_of_db` at `paired.rs:683-686,741-766`).
- **Problem & Probe:** `lift_downgrade` deletes `runner_owner_only` via `tx.lift_runner_downgrade(downgrade.epoch)` without draining `UNRECORDED_DOWNGRADE_LOG` / `UNRECORDED_DOWNGRADE_DRAINING`; `PairedRunner::policy_recheck` (`:2335-2386`) also reads `tx.runner_downgrade()` without calling `self.policy.drain_pending(...)`. **Probe (`crates/runtime/src/paired.rs`, temporary `probe_lift_downgrade_and_pending_marker`):** verified (`exit 0`) that `lift_downgrade` returned `lifted: true` while `UNRECORDED_DOWNGRADE_LOG` remained on disk, causing `RunnerPolicy::with_store` to immediately re-downgrade (`epoch 1`).
- **Smallest Fix:** Drain pending downgrade markers in `PairedRunner::policy_recheck` and `lift_downgrade` before evaluating or clearing `runner_owner_only`.
- **Test Coverage Gap:** Existing `runner_owner_only` / bridge tests do not exercise `lift_downgrade` or `policy_recheck` while an undrained `UNRECORDED_DOWNGRADE_LOG` marker is present.

### `M06-W3-R04` — `request_id` ignored by `runner_local` handlers and reused in `issuer_forms`
- **Severity:** Nonblocking (state-convergent idempotency today, but omits ADR-0006 §7 `request_id` deduplication; paired with a minor file-open hygiene issue in `read_small_file`).
- **Location:** `crates/heimdall/src/m06_cmd.rs:2589-2605` (clones one `request_id` into both `RunnerLocalRequest::TrustIssuer` and `RunnerLocalRequest::DesignateTimeSource`); `crates/runtime/src/runner.rs:405-447`; `crates/runtime/src/paired.rs:1148,2205`. Also `crates/heimdall/src/m06_cmd.rs:3425` (`read_small_file` uses `File::open` instead of `input_file::open_regular`, unlike `read_bundle` at `:2307` and `service_cmd.rs:134`).
- **Smallest Fix:** Generate distinct `request_id` values per RPC in `issuer_forms`, persist/deduplicate by `request_id` in `runner_local` where required by ADR-0006 §7, and switch `read_small_file` to `input_file::open_regular`.
- **Test Coverage Gap:** No test submits duplicate vs distinct `request_id` replay semantics across `runner_local` forms or symlink rejection on `read_small_file`.
